Thumbnail crop guide

All cursor preview thumbnails should be 560 × 315px (16:9 ratio). The preview card in the demo is 280px wide, so 560px gives you a crisp 2× retina image.

For project thumbnails with centered characters (Kyzen avatar), crop to include the full character with equal negative space left/right. For game environments (Final Score), use the full frame — the compositions are already cinematic.
